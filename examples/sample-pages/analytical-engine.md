Charles Babbage's proposed general-purpose mechanical computer (1837). Never built
in his lifetime, but its design already contained an arithmetic unit, conditional
branching, loops and memory — the structure of a modern CPU.

Documented and extended by [[ada-lovelace]], whose notes were longer than
Babbage's original paper.
