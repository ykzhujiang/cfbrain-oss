19th-century mathematician, widely regarded as the first computer programmer. Wrote
the first published algorithm intended to be carried out by a machine — Note G for
Charles Babbage's [[analytical-engine]].

Her key insight was that the machine could operate on arbitrary symbols, not just
numbers, which is the conceptual leap from calculator to computer.
